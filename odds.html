How the odds are made

  • Each player's handicap is strokes over family par across 18 holes. A scratch player (0) plays to par.
  • Pairs: Better Ball counts 70% of the lower handicap and 30% of the higher. Scramble uses the same split at half size, like the standard scramble formula.
  • The handicap gap between the sides sets each hole's chance of a win, a halve or a loss. Odds then come from every way the 18 holes can still play out, so they move as holes are entered.
  • Cup odds combine all 11 matches. Settled matches are locked in. Matches with players not yet picked use the team's average handicap and show "Provisional".
  • Player odds: the rest of the cup is played out 4,000 times. Each player earns the points of their matches (1 for a win, half for a halve). "Top scorer" is the share of runs where they finish with the most points, with dead heats split.
  • Odds are decimal: a stake times the odds is the total return, stake included.
